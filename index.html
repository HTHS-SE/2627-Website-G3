Aerospace Engineering

What is Aerospace Engineering?

Aerospace engineering is the design, development, testing, and production of aircraft. Its two branches include aeronautical engineering, which involves aircraft within the Earth's atmosphere, as well as astronautical engineering, which involves aircraft in outer space. It uses physics, mathematics, and materials science to engineer solutions for achieving flight.

Emerging Technologies

JETZERO

A 250-passenger commercial airliner being developed to use up to maximize efficiency and use 50% less fuel than current planes.
For more information, visit JetZero.

NASA X-59 QueSST

A quiet supersonic aircraft being developed as part of NASA's Quesst mission that successfully reached its target speed on June 5, 2026.
The aircraft is designed to fly faster than the speed of sound without creating supersonic booms, in hopes of allowing supersonic commercial flight.
For more information, visit NASA's QueSST Program.

DARPA X-65

A research plane being designed to use active flow control (controlling flight using pressurized gas source) to eliminate moving wing flaps.
The X-65 entered its final assembly phase in 2026.
For more information, visit DARPA's CRANE program.
